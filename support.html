Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore a purchase?

If you've already bought Premium but it isn't showing up — for example after reinstalling the app or switching to a new device — you can restore it:

  1. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Premium.
  2. Open Hikari AI and go to the Premium or Settings screen.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID, so restoring works as long as the original purchase was made with that account. If it still doesn't appear, email us at support@codewithaijoe.com.

How does the free daily AI limit work?

Free accounts include a set number of AI requests each day, shared across features like the “Ask Anything” chat, the Tutor camera, and AI-generated quizzes and lessons. Your daily count resets automatically every day, so you'll get a fresh batch the next day. If you reach the limit, you can either wait for it to reset or upgrade to Premium for a much higher daily allowance.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time:

  1. Open Hikari AI and go to the account or Settings screen.
  2. Look for the Delete Account option and follow the prompts.

If you can't find it or would prefer that we handle it for you, email us at support@codewithaijoe.com and we'll permanently delete your account and personal data. For details on what we store and how it's handled, see our Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through Apple, so they're managed in your device settings rather than inside the app:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  3. Select Hikari AI.
  4. From there you can change your plan or tap Cancel Subscription.

If you cancel, you'll keep Premium until the end of your current billing period. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
