Privacy Policy

For Hikari AI · Last updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hikari AI ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app. Hikari AI is developed and operated by Wenhe Luo, operating as CodewithJoe.

By using Hikari AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who We Are

Hikari AI is a trivia, quiz, and AI-powered learning application for mobile devices. The person responsible for your data ("data controller") is:

Wenhe Luo (CodewithJoe)
Contact: support@codewithaijoe.com

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in using Sign in with Apple, we receive a unique identifier for your account. If you choose to share them, we may also receive your name and email address. Apple gives you the option to hide your email address, in which case we receive a private relay email address instead of your real one. We use this information only to create and secure your account.

Profile Information

You may set a username and choose an avatar within the app. This information is stored as part of your account.

Content You Provide

Hikari AI includes AI features. When you use them, the following content is collected and processed to generate a response:

  • Chat messages: text you type into the "Ask Anything" AI chat.
  • Camera images: photos you capture using the Tutor camera feature so the AI can analyze them and provide help.
  • Quiz and lesson topics: the subjects or prompts you request for AI-generated quizzes and lessons.

Usage Information

We record the number of AI requests you make each day. This is used to enforce daily usage limits for free and premium accounts. We do not use this information for advertising.

Subscription Information

If you purchase a premium subscription, the transaction is handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation of your subscription status from Apple, but we do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple processes all payments.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, secure, and manage your account.
  • Provide the app's core features, including AI-generated chat, quizzes, lessons, and camera-based help.
  • Enforce daily AI usage limits and distinguish free from premium accounts.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent abuse or misuse of the service.

4. How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers necessary to operate the app:

Supabase

We use Supabase to host our backend database and manage authentication. Your account information, profile, and usage records are stored using Supabase. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Groq

Our AI features are powered by Groq. When you use the AI chat, camera, quizzes, or lessons, the relevant content (your text messages, camera images, or requested topics) is sent to Groq's API to generate a response. See Groq's Privacy Policy.

Apple

We use Apple's services for sign-in and for processing subscription payments through the App Store. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will remove your personal information from our systems, except where we are required to retain it for legal or legitimate operational reasons. Content sent to AI providers to generate responses is handled according to those providers' own policies, linked above.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may request to access or delete the personal information associated with your account at any time. To do so, contact us at support@codewithaijoe.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (Europe) or CCPA (California), including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. We honor these rights for all users.

7. Children's Privacy

Hikari AI is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@codewithaijoe.com and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including secure authentication and encrypted connections to our backend services. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside of your country of residence, including in the United States. By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations.
